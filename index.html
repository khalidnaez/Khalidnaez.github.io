Auto pilot uploader brings AI-assisted video preparation, review, TikTok draft delivery, and performance insights into one local desktop workflow.

Development stage · Authorized Sandbox testers

From an idea to an informed next episode.

01 / REVIEW

Watch before you send.

Prepare a video with your AI automation, review the result in your local dashboard, and explicitly approve the content for upload.

02 / FINISH IN TIKTOK

Keep control of publishing.

The intended integration sends approved videos through TikTok’s official draft-upload API. Open the inbox notification in TikTok, edit, add the caption, and decide whether to publish.

03 / LEARN

Use available public metrics.

After publication and authorization, retrieve available views, likes, comments, and shares for your own public videos to inform future content planning.

Built around explicit permission.

TikTok authorization is requested through OAuth. The integration requests basic profile information, permission to upload drafts, and access to public video metadata. It does not ask for your TikTok password in the local dashboard.

The confirmed operating policy keeps application data on the operator’s Windows computer, limits what is sent to AI providers, and provides an email route for deletion requests.
