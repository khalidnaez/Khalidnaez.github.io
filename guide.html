How the workflow works.

The following describes the intended integration. Setup and live API testing are still in progress.

1. Connect your account

Start authorization in the local Windows dashboard. TikTok presents the requested permissions. A Desktop OAuth callback returns to http://localhost:8765/tiktok/callback. Desktop authentication uses an anti-forgery state and PKCE. Only authorized Sandbox target users can use the development configuration.

2. Review and approve

Watch the prepared video and check rights, accuracy, captions, and any required AI-generated or commercial-content disclosures. Approval is required before transferring a video to TikTok. A connection action must not silently upload an existing queue without a clear, explicit upload approval.

3. Finish publishing in TikTok

Successful inbox delivery means TikTok has sent a notification to continue its editing flow. It does not mean the video has been published or is guaranteed to appear as an ordinary saved draft. Open the notification and finish the post in the TikTok app. You retain the final publishing decision.

4. Inspect performance

Once a video is public and available to the API, the application can request its view, like, comment, and share counts. Drafts and private videos are not covered by the public-video listing permission. Matching an uploaded episode to a published video requires a reliable post identifier or explicit confirmation; it is not assumed from timing alone.

Disconnect or delete

Revoke the application’s access in TikTok and request local record deletion by emailing khalidnaeth@gmail.com. The operator handles requests within 30 days. Deleting application records does not remove a published TikTok post; manage those posts in TikTok.

Troubleshooting

IssueAction
Redirect mismatchThe authorization request, token request, and registered Desktop callback must match exactly.
Permission deniedConfirm the scope is active and was granted by the account. Reauthorize when scopes change.
Pending-share limitPause uploads and resolve pending content in TikTok. Do not retry repeatedly.
Token expired or revokedRefresh securely where permitted; otherwise reconnect. Do not log tokens.
Rate limit or server errorUse bounded retries and backoff. Reconcile upload status before initializing another upload.
