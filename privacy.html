Privacy Policy

Auto pilot uploader is operated by Khaled Albostah as an individual developer. Contact: khalidnaeth@gmail.com.

This policy defines the approved operating rules for the development-stage application and its authorized testing. The local integration is still being implemented and validated. Features described below operate only when enabled and authorized; publishing this policy is not a claim that all functionality has been deployed.

Information and purposes

  • Account connection: TikTok OAuth identifiers, granted scopes, access tokens, refresh tokens, basic display name, and avatar when requested are used to identify and maintain the authorized connection.
  • Content: Videos, captions, approval decisions, upload identifiers, and processing status are used to prepare content and send explicitly approved videos to TikTok’s inbox workflow.
  • Performance: Public video identifiers and available view, like, comment, and share counts are used to display performance and improve content planning. The app does not request comment text, follower lists, direct messages, or private-video access.
  • Support: Emails and relevant details provided by a requester are used to answer support and deletion requests.
  • Operational records: Minimal timestamps, outcome codes, and redacted error details are used for troubleshooting. Credentials and authorization codes must not be included in logs.

Storage and retention

The approved application configuration stores videos, tokens, and metrics locally on the operator’s Windows computer. Secrets must be excluded from source control and protected using local access controls and an appropriate secret store. Automated retention enforcement has not yet been verified; the operator is responsible for enforcing these periods, including by manual deletion during testing.

RecordRetention rule
Uploaded local video copiesDelete within 30 days after upload, or earlier on request.
Performance metricsUp to 12 months.
Operational logsUp to 30 days.
OAuth credentials and connection recordsOnly while the connection is active and necessary; remove locally when disconnected.

Unuploaded content may be kept until uploaded, cancelled, or deleted by its owner. No separate cloud backup is part of the approved application storage configuration.

Sharing and service providers

Explicitly approved content and the credentials needed to perform authorized API requests are transmitted to TikTok. TikTok processes that information under its own terms and privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts this public website and may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and technical logs under GitHub’s policies. This site sets no application tracking cookies and includes no advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

Claude, provided by Anthropic, may receive selected performance counts and the operator’s own content-planning information for inference and suggestions. TikTok OAuth tokens, authorization codes, and TikTok profile information must not be sent to Claude. Other generation providers may process creator-supplied inputs when separately configured; those providers and inputs must be disclosed before use. Third-party processing may occur outside your country.

AI use restrictions

TikTok data must not be used to train or fine-tune AI models. Any use of selected metrics for AI-assisted planning is conditional on TikTok’s applicable developer terms and the provider’s permitted data-use settings. If that use is not permitted, keep the metrics in the local dashboard and omit them from AI prompts.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline permissions and revoke access through TikTok’s connected-app controls. To request access, correction, or deletion of locally held information, email khalidnaeth@gmail.com from an address you control and identify the relevant account without sending passwords or tokens. The operator may request proportionate verification and handles requests within 30 days. Deleting local records does not delete content already held by TikTok or change third-party retention practices. Delete TikTok posts in TikTok.

Security, children, and changes

The service is intended for adult operators and authorized adult testers. It is not directed at children. No system can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to the data practices will be reflected here before the changed processing is introduced. Applicable privacy rights remain available regardless of these operating defaults.
